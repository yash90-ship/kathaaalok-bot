# kathaaalok content & safety rules (every reel must follow these)

## Instagram account safety
- Post only through the official Instagram Graph API. Never log in with a password, never use unofficial tools.
- Max 2 posts per day (13:00 and 20:00 IST). Never post more.
- No automated likes, follows, comments, DMs or story views. Ever.
- 3-5 relevant hashtags only (Instagram's limit). No repeated identical captions.
- Every reel is approved by the owner on Telegram before posting (until approval mode is switched off).

## Original content only
- Every reel is newly written and newly drawn in our own paper-cutout style. No reposts, no clips or
  images from other pages, films or TV, no other creators' voices.
- Do not copy another page's characters, logos or branding.

## Music: copyright-free only
- Use ONLY music we generate ourselves, or tracks with a licence that allows commercial use on Instagram
  without attribution or claims (CC0 / public domain). Keep the licence file next to every track in assets/music/.
- Never use film songs, Bollywood/bhajan recordings, trending Instagram audio or anything with unclear rights.
- Music matches the story's mood (devotional, dramatic, battle, festive) and stays under the narration.

## Religious content
- Stay faithful to the cited scripture; name the source on screen. If versions differ, say so.
- Fact-check every script before rendering. No invented events presented as scripture.
- Respectful depiction of all deities and traditions. No mockery, no hate, no attacks on any religion,
  caste, community or person. No political content. No graphic violence or gore.
- No medical, financial or superstitious claims presented as guaranteed ("do this and you will get rich").

## Format
- 9:16, 1080x1920. About 60 s; bigger stories up to 90 s, never longer.
- Subtitles inside the Instagram safe area (not in the top ~250 px or bottom ~400 px).

## Growth: reach and followers (goal: reach as many people as possible)
- Topics: only what people are searching for right now (festivals in the next 1-5 days, trending
  mythology questions, films/series in the news). Before writing, collect real search phrases
  (Google/YouTube autocomplete + Google Trends) in Hindi, Hinglish and English.
- Hook (first 1-2 s): a curiosity question or a surprising fact people relate to
  ("साँझ को तुलसी क्यों नहीं तोड़ते?"). The hook is also the first line of the caption and the cover text.
- Keywords everywhere Instagram reads them: spoken narration, on-screen subtitles, caption first lines.
  Use the exact phrases people search ("तुलसी विवाह कथा", "tulsi vivah story", "shiv puran katha").
- Caption: hook line -> a line of Hindi keywords -> a line of HINGLISH keywords (AT LEAST 12 keywords in total, specific to the video; separate keywords with commas) (Roman-script Hindi as people
  type it: "shiv ji ki katha", "tulsi vivah kyu hota hai") -> a relatable question -> follow line.
- Hashtags (3-5): 1 broad (#hindumythology), 1-2 niche (#shivpuran), 1 trending/festival (#devdeepawali),
  1 brand (#kathaaalok). Never banned or unrelated tags.
- Engagement: end with a devotional comment prompt ("हर हर महादेव लिखिए"), make reels people want to
  share with family and save; use "Part 1 / Part 2" series for big stories.
- Learn weekly from Insights (views, shares, saves, follows) and repeat what works.
- Never use clickbait that the video doesn't deliver, fake claims, or misleading titles.
